About Bass Reflections

Our mission is simple: spread the joy of Bass Music by playing tracks we love.

We're passionate about the full spectrum of bass-driven electronic music, from the funky vibes of 90s Florida Breaks and the raw energy of early 2000s NuSkool Breaks and Dubstep; all the way to modern Wave, Trap, Midtempo, and Drum & Bass.

If you know me as Sub Reflux, Sky Drone, Bass Reflex, or Bass Reflections from past communities like Ravetrax, BreaksFM, iBreaks, AfterHoursDJs or NSBRadio; welcome back and thank you for sticking around through the years.

Tune in. Turn it up. This is Bass Reflections.
